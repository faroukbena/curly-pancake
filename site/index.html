WOLO-PURE

A cooperative family game for short daily routines, weekly play and year-round curiosity about the heritages in your household.

Open the appPrint the kit

How it works

  • Play together: every step is shared, and passing or listening is always fine.
  • Learn gently: reviews are reminders you can adjust or skip.
  • Keep stories safe: private by default, with consent, evidence labels and withdrawal.
  • Optional faith modules; no assumptions about family shape or beliefs.

Honest status

Current build is a local demo: data stays in your browser, there are no accounts or syncing, and prompts are illustrative placeholders with no cultural facts. Spaced review timing is a product hypothesis to validate with families.

Privacy

No tracking, no public profiles, no leaderboards. Nothing leaves your device in this demo.
