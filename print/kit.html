Facilitator guide & rules

  1. Short game (15 min): take turns drawing a card; do, discuss, or pass. Listen-only is always allowed.
  2. Any contribution moves the shared token one step. Nobody is ranked; no penalties.
  3. Review one earlier item, then choose what to revisit.
  4. Expanded game (45 min): add a team challenge with more than one way to succeed, and a family-approved addition to the collection (with consent).
